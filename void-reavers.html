VOID REAVERS

Frontier Patrol · Sector Theta-9Full screen, without Safari's bars: Share → Add to Home Screen

You fly the patrol cutter SRV Halcyon. The reaver clans, alien pirates led by a brain in a jar called the Cerebrant, are raiding the stations of Theta-9. Chase its flagship across two sectors. In each one, clear the packs and guard the station (it scores while it lives, and if it falls you fly the sector again). Drive the flagship off in the Drift, then destroy it in Ember Reach.

Your station has a small defence laser. Watch for a disabled fighter drifting in the sector: park alongside her and she'll fly as your escort until she's shot down.

Your cutter starts with its shields dead: one hit downs it. You eject in a space suit with a single thruster and no brakes, and have to fly yourself back to the station for a new cutter. Get hit or run out of air on the way, and the run is over. Clear the first sector and the yard crews rebuild the shields: from then on every cutter has three shield cells. Each soaks a hit, and they charge back when you stay out of the fire for a few seconds. The cutter flies like a spaceship: it glides a long way after every burn, so start your stops early. And Theta-9 is only the beginning. Laser shreds light hulls and is the only thing that breaks asteroids. Don't crash into them. You carry a rack of ten missiles: fire them in a fast salvo, and they reload one by one after a short pause. A missile cracks shields; the laser only wears them down. Wardens and the flagship shoot missiles down, so swamp them with a salvo. Don't hug a reaver when it blows.
