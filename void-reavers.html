VOID REAVERS

Frontier Patrol · Sector Theta-9

You fly the patrol cutter SRV Halcyon. The reaver clans, alien pirates led by a brain in a jar called the Cerebrant, are raiding the stations of Theta-9. Chase its flagship across three sectors. In each one, clear the packs, guard the station (it scores while it lives, and if it falls you fly the sector again), and drive the flagship off. Then follow it into the Cradle: no station, just hordes, and the flagship's last stand.

Your station has a small defence laser. Watch for a disabled fighter drifting in the sector: park alongside her and she'll fly as your escort until she's shot down.

No shields: one hit downs your cutter, but Command launches a backup and your progress holds. Laser shreds light hulls and is the only thing that breaks asteroids. Don't crash into them. A missile cracks shielded gunships, but wardens and the flagship shoot missiles down. Don't hug a reaver when it blows.
