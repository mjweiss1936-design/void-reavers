# Void Reavers

A single-file space shooter: one HTML page with a canvas and synthesized Web Audio. It has no build step, no dependencies and no sound files.

## Play

**Online:** https://mjweiss1936-design.github.io/void-reavers/void-reavers.html

Or open `void-reavers.html` in a browser.

## The campaign

The cutter flies like a spaceship: it glides a long way after every burn and slows only gently, so start your stops early. The retro thrusters stop you faster.

In the first sector your cutter's shields are dead, so one hit destroys it. Clear that sector and the yard crews rebuild the shields: from then on every cutter has **three shield cells**. Each cell soaks one hit, and they charge back when you stay out of the fire for a few seconds.

When a cutter is destroyed, you eject in a space suit with a single thruster, no brakes and 60 seconds of air, and must fly back to the station for a new cutter. If you're hit or run out of air on the way, the run is over.

Part one has two sectors. In each, defend a station from Reaver packs, stop beacon jobs before their 45-second fuse runs out, and take on the clan flagship. In the Drift it runs once it's hurt; in Ember Reach you destroy it.

Win and you're awarded the Star of Theta-9 on the carrier's command deck. Then the campaign keeps going.

### Part two: the Matriarch

The Cerebrant was only a steward. Partway through the medal ceremony, a whole fleet of flagships drops out of warp, and their boarding vessels latch onto the carrier.

- **Boarders:** a fight on foot along the Resolute's core deck, seen from above at a slight angle. You have a rifle and twenty marines, and they move and fight as one squad around you. The boarders come aboard stern to bow, so you clear the ship one way and never double back:
  - The first wave cuts in at engineering, by the engines.
  - The second wave hits the crew deck and the reactor. A green arrow shows you where to move up to between waves.
  - The last wave comes in everywhere at once, the bow first. That's when your squad splits up: fire teams dig in at the engines and the reactor, and the rest of the marines go with you to the bridge.

  Boarders at a system don't just stand there. Squids jolt it with arcs of current, blobs spit acid into it, and brutes beat on it. Some plant a charge on it that ticks faster and faster before it blows a chunk out: shoot it off. Lose any one system and the ship is lost.

  Your squad's lasers hit instantly, in a big flash. Your marines are tough, and most of them make it to the end if you keep them in the fight. The boarders' bolts are slower, and you can jump and roll clear of them. Your own rifle hits hard: it knocks boarders back, and they wobble and pop like water balloons. If the boarders destroy the engines or the reactor, the Resolute blows up around you, and then you watch her go from outside.

  Hold the Resolute and her armorers refit your cutter: **proton torpedoes** and **five shield cells** from here on (see below).
- **Earth:** Earth sits just below Skyhook Station with the Moon beyond it, and the rest of the solar system's planets are out across the sector. Defend the station through three hordes, with half of each horde going for the station. All the while, the fleets are at war around you (see below). When the last horde breaks, the fleet strips the shield off the Matriarch's flagship. Then you silence its four gun batteries, with lasers, missiles or torpedoes, and leave the hull in one piece.
- **Boarding:** Skyhook's personnel carrier brings a fresh squad of marines and clamps onto the crippled flagship's hull at its stern. Take them through it, chamber by chamber toward the bow. Each chamber has a nerve node feeding its brood pits: burn the node out and those pits go dead, so nothing comes at you from behind. With all three gone, the membrane over the helm opens. Then hold the helm while the ship throws everything it has at you. Get knocked down three times and the boarding fails.
- **Broodhome:** fly the captured flagship to the clans' homeworld, with the Home Fleet jumping in behind you. It has eight shield cells, a three-gun broadside, twin torpedo tubes and its own point defence, but it turns slowly. Survive two hordes. Then knock out the three spore pylons feeding the shield on the Matriarch's ark, and the whole fleet turns its lances on her.

If the carrier falls or the boarding fails, you play that fight again from the start.

### Proton torpedoes

From Earth on, you have a third weapon: blue proton torpedoes (**3** on a keyboard, **TORP** on a phone). From there the war is yours to win. Only real carelessness gets you killed.

- **Homing:** a torpedo homes on whatever's in front of you, big things first, and nothing of hers can shoot one down.
- **What they kill:**
  - The blast wipes out every reaver in it, shields and all, along with their bolts and missiles.
  - One torpedo kills one of her gun-beasts, and two kill one of her line's cruisers, even the ones fighting out in the background.
  - One torpedo silences a battery on her flagship or knocks out a pylon round the ark. Once the ark's shield is down, torpedoes burn the ark itself.
  - They never hurt you or our side.
- **The rack:** it holds six. It starts reloading a moment after your last shot, one torpedo every two and a half seconds.
- **The flagship:** the captured flagship fires them in pairs.
- **Shields:** her own flagship's shield and the ark's still hold until the fleet strips them or the pylons fall. A torpedo just splashes off.

### The fleets at war

Over Earth and over Broodhome, a line of Earth's warships (the Resolute and her destroyers) and a line of the Matriarch's (her purple line flagships and gun-beasts) fight all sector long. You're the fighter weaving through it: the reavers are yours, and the big guns are theirs.

- **Shells** cross the gap between the lines. Ours, the cyan ones, pass you by; hers, the pink ones, hit you if one catches you square.
- **Lances:** before a warship fires its lance, its lane lights up as a dashed line for about a second and a half. Get out of it: a lance cuts down anything in its lane, you included, from either side.
- **Cover:** our warships' flak picks off reavers that come close, and their hulls stop the reavers' bolts. Fly near one of hers and her guns turn on you.
- **Wrecks:** warships burn and break in two, and the halves drift and soak up fire. Fresh ships jump in to fill the gaps.
- **Bonus:** every one of our warships still in the line when her ship falls is worth 1,000 points.

## On a phone

Touch screens get a touch mode, played in landscape:

- **Left thumb:** touch anywhere on the left half and drag. A small push turns the cutter to aim, and a big push burns the engine that way.
- **Right thumb:** hold **LASER** or **MISSILE** to fire, and slide between them without lifting your thumb. From Earth on, **TORP** (above MISSILE) fires proton torpedoes. Tap **BRAKE** to come to a dead stop, or hold it to slow down.
- **Corner map:** tap it for the full sector map, and tap anywhere to put it away.
- **☰:** the menu (see below).
- **Everything's small:** a phone shows about as much of the sector as a desktop monitor does, with small buttons and a trimmed HUD (just your score, your shields and the mission), so the screen is left to the fight. On a short screen, like Safari's under its toolbar and tab bar, the buttons and the cutscene radio get smaller still.
- **On foot:** push the left thumb the way you want to run, and hold **FIRE** to shoot. Your rifle finds the nearest boarder for you, or a charge first if one's ticking. Tap **ROLL** (where MISSILE is in space) to jump and roll the way you're running, clear of their bolts. Slide your thumb onto it from FIRE and you keep shooting as you go.
- **Cutscenes:** tap to move the dialogue on, or **SKIP** to jump ahead.

Holding the phone upright pauses the game and asks you to turn it sideways. On iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen plays it full screen, without Safari's toolbar and tab bar; in Safari, the title card says so under the title. The game keeps the page from zooming, but if it ever does get zoomed in, the game pauses and asks you to pinch back out. Sound needs the silent switch off.

Add `?touch` to the address to try touch mode with a mouse.

## The menu

**☰**, or **P** / **Esc**, opens a small menu. In a fight it pauses the game, and it shows your time, score and cutters lost. From it:
- **Resume** (or tap outside the menu)
- **Sector map**
- **Jump to…** any part of the campaign
- **Music** and **Sound effects**, on or off

## Jumping around

**B**, the menu's **Jump to…**, or a cutscene's **SKIP** button opens the jump menu. From it you can go straight to:
- the intro
- the Drift's start, its flagship fight, or the jump scene after it
- Ember Reach's start, its flagship fight, the medal ceremony, or the fleet arriving
- part two: the fight for the carrier, the jump to Earth, any Earth horde, the Earth flagship, boarding it, seizing it, the flight to Broodhome, the Matriarch, and the ending

The green button at the top finishes the part you're in. It skips the scene, clears the sector's packs, knocks the flagship down to its last hit (over Earth, all but its last battery; over Broodhome, her shield gone and the ark on its last hit), or wins the fight on foot. Pressing B twice does the same thing.

## On foot

Arrows or **WASD** move. Hold **Space** to fire: your rifle finds the nearest boarder you can see, or a charge on a machine first. Or aim with the mouse and hold the button. **Shift** or a right-click jumps you up and over in a roll the way you're running, and their bolts go straight past you while you're in it. It needs a moment to recharge. A boarder about to fire at you swells a red bolt in its mouth first. Your marines follow you, so take them where the fight is. On the Resolute's last wave, fire teams peel off to hold the engines and the reactor.

**F** calls in a 45-ship fleet.
