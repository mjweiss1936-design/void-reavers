# Void Reavers

A single-file space shooter: one HTML page with a canvas and synthesized Web Audio. It has no build step, no dependencies and no sound files.

## Play

**Online:** https://mjweiss1936-design.github.io/void-reavers/void-reavers.html

Or open `void-reavers.html` in a browser.

## The campaign

The cutter flies like a spaceship: it glides a long way after every burn and slows only gently, so start your stops early. The retro thrusters stop you faster.

In the first sector your cutter's shields are dead, so one hit destroys it. Clear that sector and the yard crews rebuild the shields: from then on every cutter has **three shield cells**. Each cell soaks one hit, and they charge back when you stay out of the fire for a few seconds.

When a cutter is destroyed, you eject in a space suit with a single thruster, no brakes and 60 seconds of air, and must fly back to the station for a new cutter. If you're hit or run out of air on the way, the run is over.

Part one has two sectors. In each, defend a station from Reaver packs, stop beacon jobs before their 45-second fuse runs out, and take on the clan flagship. In the Drift it runs once it's hurt; in Ember Reach you destroy it.

Win and you're awarded the Star of Theta-9 on the carrier's command deck. Then the campaign keeps going.

### Part two: the Matriarch

The Cerebrant was only a steward. Partway through the medal ceremony, a whole fleet of flagships drops out of warp, and their boarding vessels latch onto the carrier.

- **Boarders:** a fight on foot across the Resolute's core deck, seen from above at a slight angle. You have a rifle and twenty marines, and they move and fight as one squad around you. Three waves of boarders cut in through the hull and go for the bridge, the reactor and the engines. Lose any one of them and the ship is lost. Your squad's lasers hit instantly; the boarders' bolts are slower, and you can dive clear of them. Your own rifle hits hard: it knocks boarders back, and they wobble and pop like water balloons. Expect only two or three of your marines to make it to the end. If the boarders destroy the engines or the reactor, the Resolute blows up around you, and then you watch her go from outside.
- **Earth:** Earth sits just below Skyhook Station with the Moon beyond it, and the rest of the solar system's planets are out across the sector. Defend the station through three hordes, with half of each horde going for the station. All the while, the fleets are at war around you (see below). When the last horde breaks, the fleet strips the shield off the Matriarch's flagship. Then you silence its four gun batteries, with lasers or missiles, and leave the hull in one piece.
- **Boarding:** Skyhook's personnel carrier brings a fresh squad of marines and clamps onto the crippled flagship's hull. Take them through it. Burn out its three nerve nodes, and the membrane over the helm opens. Then hold the helm while the ship throws everything it has at you. Get knocked down three times and the boarding fails.
- **Broodhome:** fly the captured flagship to the clans' homeworld, with the Home Fleet jumping in behind you. It has six shield cells, a three-gun broadside and its own point defence, but it turns slowly. Survive two hordes. Then knock out the three spore pylons feeding the shield on the Matriarch's ark, and the whole fleet turns its lances on her.

If the carrier falls or the boarding fails, you play that fight again from the start.

### The fleets at war

Over Earth and over Broodhome, a line of Earth's warships (the Resolute and her destroyers) and a line of the Matriarch's (her purple line flagships and gun-beasts) fight all sector long. You're the fighter weaving through it: the reavers are yours, and the big guns are theirs.

- **Shells** cross the gap between the lines. Ours, the cyan ones, pass you by; hers, the pink ones, hit you if one catches you square.
- **Lances:** before a warship fires its lance, its lane lights up as a dashed line for about a second and a half. Get out of it: a lance cuts down anything in its lane, you included, from either side.
- **Cover:** our warships' flak picks off reavers that come close, and their hulls stop the reavers' bolts. Fly near one of hers and her guns turn on you.
- **Wrecks:** warships burn and break in two, and the halves drift and soak up fire. Fresh ships jump in to fill the gaps.
- **Bonus:** every one of our warships still in the line when her ship falls is worth 1,000 points.

## On a phone

Touch screens get a touch mode, played in landscape:

- **Left thumb:** touch anywhere on the left half and drag. A small push turns the cutter to aim, and a big push burns the engine that way.
- **Right thumb:** hold **LASER** or **MISSILE** to fire, and slide between them without lifting your thumb. Tap **BRAKE** to come to a dead stop, or hold it to slow down.
- **Corner map:** tap it for the full sector map, and tap anywhere to put it away.
- **☰:** the menu (see below).
- **Everything's small:** a phone shows about as much of the sector as a desktop monitor does, with small buttons and a trimmed HUD (just your score, your shields and the mission), so the screen is left to the fight. On a short screen, like Safari's under its toolbar and tab bar, the buttons and the cutscene radio get smaller still.
- **On foot:** push the left thumb the way you want to run, and hold **FIRE** to shoot. Your rifle finds the nearest boarder for you. Tap **DASH** (where MISSILE is in space) to dive the way you're running, clear of their bolts. Slide your thumb onto it from FIRE and you keep shooting as you go.
- **Cutscenes:** tap to move the dialogue on, or **SKIP** to jump ahead.

Holding the phone upright pauses the game and asks you to turn it sideways. On iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen plays it full screen, without Safari's toolbar and tab bar; in Safari, the title card says so under the title. The game keeps the page from zooming, but if it ever does get zoomed in, the game pauses and asks you to pinch back out. Sound needs the silent switch off.

Add `?touch` to the address to try touch mode with a mouse.

## The menu

**☰**, or **P** / **Esc**, opens a small menu. In a fight it pauses the game, and it shows your time, score and cutters lost. From it:
- **Resume** (or tap outside the menu)
- **Sector map**
- **Jump to…** any part of the campaign
- **Music** and **Sound effects**, on or off

## Jumping around

**B**, the menu's **Jump to…**, or a cutscene's **SKIP** button opens the jump menu. From it you can go straight to:
- the intro
- the Drift's start, its flagship fight, or the jump scene after it
- Ember Reach's start, its flagship fight, the medal ceremony, or the fleet arriving
- part two: the fight for the carrier, the jump to Earth, any Earth horde, the Earth flagship, boarding it, seizing it, the flight to Broodhome, the Matriarch, and the ending

The green button at the top finishes the part you're in. It skips the scene, clears the sector's packs, knocks the flagship down to its last hit (over Earth, all but its last battery; over Broodhome, her shield gone and the ark on its last hit), or wins the fight on foot. Pressing B twice does the same thing.

## On foot

Arrows or **WASD** move. Hold **Space** to fire: your rifle finds the nearest boarder you can see. Or aim with the mouse and hold the button. **Shift** or a right-click dives you the way you're running, and their bolts go straight past you while you're in it. It needs a moment to recharge. A boarder about to fire at you swells a red bolt in its mouth first. Your marines follow you, so take them where the fight is.

**F** calls in a 45-ship fleet.
