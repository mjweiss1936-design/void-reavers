# Void Reavers

A single-file space shooter: one HTML page with a canvas and synthesized Web Audio. It has no build step, no dependencies and no sound files.

## Play

**Online:** https://mjweiss1936-design.github.io/void-reavers/void-reavers.html

Or open `void-reavers.html` in a browser.

## The campaign

The cutter flies like a spaceship: it glides a long way after every burn and slows only gently, so start your stops early. The retro thrusters stop you faster.

In the first sector your cutter's shields are dead, so one hit destroys it. Clear that sector and the yard crews rebuild the shields: from then on every cutter has **three shield cells**. Each cell soaks one hit, and they charge back when you stay out of the fire for a few seconds.

When a cutter is destroyed, you eject in a space suit with a single thruster, no brakes and 60 seconds of air, and must fly back to the station for a new cutter. If you're hit or run out of air on the way, the run is over.

There are four sectors:
- **The first three:** defend a station from Reaver packs, stop beacon jobs before their 45-second fuse runs out, and fight off the clan flagship.
- **The fourth, the Cradle:** there's no station. Three escort fighters fly on your wing while you survive three hordes, and the carrier replaces any you lose between hordes. Then the flagship makes its last stand.

Win and you're awarded the Star of Theta-9 on the carrier's command deck.

## On a phone

Touch screens get a touch mode, played in landscape:

- **Left thumb:** touch anywhere on the left half and drag. A small push turns the cutter to aim, and a big push burns the engine that way.
- **Right thumb:** hold **LASER** or **MISSILE** to fire, and slide between them without lifting your thumb. Tap **BRAKE** to come to a dead stop, or hold it to slow down.
- **Corner map:** tap it for the full sector map, and tap anywhere to put it away.
- **☰:** the menu (see below).
- **Everything's small:** a phone shows about as much of the sector as a desktop monitor does, with small buttons and a trimmed HUD (just your score, your shields and the mission), so the screen is left to the fight.
- **Cutscenes:** tap to move the dialogue on, or **SKIP** to jump ahead.

Holding the phone upright pauses the game and asks you to turn it sideways. On iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen plays it full screen. Sound needs the silent switch off.

Add `?touch` to the address to try touch mode with a mouse.

## The menu

**☰**, or **P** / **Esc**, opens a small menu. In a fight it pauses the game, and it shows your time, score and cutters lost. From it:
- **Resume** (or tap outside the menu)
- **Sector map**
- **Jump to…** any part of the campaign
- **Music** and **Sound effects**, on or off

## Jumping around

**B**, the menu's **Jump to…**, or a cutscene's **SKIP** button opens the jump menu. From it you can go straight to:
- the intro
- any sector's start, flagship fight, or jump scene
- any Cradle horde
- the ending: the flight home or the medal ceremony

The green button at the top finishes the part you're in. It skips the scene, clears the sector's packs, or knocks the flagship down to its last hit. Pressing B twice does the same thing.

**F** calls in a 45-ship fleet.
